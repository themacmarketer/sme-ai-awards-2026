The SME AI Impact Awards celebrate and recognise SMEs that have achieved business benefits, growth and digital transformation through Al. This award, jointly organised by IMDA and SBF, is part of the National AI Impact Programme and is in support of Singapore's National Al Strategy.

This year, the Awards recognise 30 SMEs who have achieved business impact and results with Al.

Frequently asked questions

Can a company apply for both award categories?

No, a company can only apply for one award category. We recommend selecting the category that best reflects your strongest business impact.

Can I apply if my company received grant support for the AI solution?

Yes. The SME AI Impact Awards is open to all SMEs who have adopted AI solutions, regardless of government programme participation or grant support. All SMEs are evaluated equally regardless of solution type or grant support received

Can a company apply if the AI solution was developed by a third-party vendor?

Yes. The SME AI Impact Awards recognises the business transformation achieved through the use of AI, regardless of whether the solution was built in-house or developed by an external vendor. What matters is how your company has adopted, integrated and leveraged the AI solution to drive measurable impact.

Do IMDA pre-approved AI solutions have any advantage during judging?

No. All SMEs are evaluated equally regardless of solution type or grant support.

Can a subsidiary of a larger company apply?

A subsidiary may still apply if it meets all of the following eligibility requirements:
Be registered and operating in Singapore.
Have at least 30% local shareholding.
Have a group annual sales turnover not exceeding S$100 million OR a group employment size not exceeding 200 employees.
Have used an AI solution for a minimum of 6 months with positive business impact.

What if my company uses multiple AI solutions?

You are welcome to highlight multiple AI solutions in your nomination. However, we encourage you to focus on one solution that has delivered the most significant and measurable business impact.

As an AI solution vendor, can I submit a nomination on behalf of my SME customers?

All nominations should come directly from SME end users. If you're a vendor or AI solution provider, you could invite your SME customers to submit their nominations.

What is the closing date of the nomination period?

The nomination will be open until the 14th August 2026, 2359hrs.

When does the 6 months of solution deployment start and end?

Your company must have used the AI solution for at least 6 months from the date your nomination is submitted.

What happens after I submit my nomination?

Once your nomination is submitted, you will receive an acknowledgement of receipt.

Your submission will be reviewed by our assessment team, and we will be in touch if your company is shortlisted for a site visit.

Final winners will be announced at the Awards ceremony on 13 October 2026. Invitation to attend the Awards ceremony will be sent closer to date. Company representatives must be present in person.

What is the purpose of the site visit? Who will conduct the site visit?

The site visit serves to verify the information provided in your nomination for a first-hand understanding of how your AI solution has been implemented and the tangible impact it has had on your business. Site visits will be conducted by SBF, the co-organiser and may include a walkthrough of your operations, a demonstration of the AI solution, and a discussion with key personnel involved in the implementation or use of the AI solution.

Will I be notified in advance of the site visit?

Yes. Shortlisted companies will be notified in advance, and the site visit will be scheduled at a mutually convenient date and time. You will be given sufficient notice to prepare accordingly. Should you need to reschedule, please contact the organising team as early as possible.

What should I prepare for the site visit?

To make the most of your site visit, we recommend preparing the following:
A brief presentation or overview of your AI solution including a live demonstration of the AI solution, where possible
Supporting documentation such as performance metrics, KPIs, and data showing measurable business impact
Key personnel who were involved in the AI implementation (e.g., project leads, operations staff) to speak to the solution's outcomes
Personnel who are actively using the AI solution to speak about how they use the AI solution and how their work scope has changed with AI

How many award winners will there be?

We will be recognising up to 30 SMEs this year.

Is my nomination information confidential?

All information disclosed to IMDA will be kept strictly confidential and used primarily for the purpose of evaluating and selecting award winners. Where applicable, selected information may also be used for marketing and publicity purposes including features on our website and media coverage. We will seek your consent before publishing any such materials.

What if I still cannot find answers to my question?

You can email us at sme_ai_awards@imda.gov.sg.

02SCQA case study

438 words
SSituation

Catalyst Performance is a personal training studio at Manulife Tower in Singapore's central business district. Bervin Manoharan, Jeremy Soh and Dr Luqman Haris founded it after corporate careers in the industry, and their coaching experience spans more than 25 years combined. The studio coaches working adults from their thirties onward, including clients over 50, athletes, and pre-natal and post-natal clients. Every client starts with a four-pillar Healthspan Assessment covering body composition, cardiorespiratory fitness, stability and strength. It is a small business with a small team, competing for local attention against larger gyms that employ marketing departments.

CComplication

The legacy website lacked search engine optimisation and generative engine optimisation infrastructure, and the studio sat on page four of Google. Website updates, content creation, advertising campaigns and lead outreach required significant time or external agency support, and the studio had no in-house digital capacity for any of it. Manoharan states that there was budget for an agency, but that no agency would promise an outcome, so the work was taken on internally instead. Growth therefore depended on buying attention or on the founders' spare hours. Marketing was intermittent, which is the worst pattern for search visibility, because gains decay when publishing stops.

QQuestion

How could a small coaching business build and run a full digital acquisition operation, and own the systems behind it, without hiring an agency or a technical team?

AAnswer

Manoharan built the systems himself: the website, the marketing systems, the operations panel, the WhatsApp and Instagram assistants, and the client nutrition log. Each is built around how the studio coaches, and each uses whichever model does the job best. The company release names DeepSeek and Fable, describing the latter as the latest Claude model from Anthropic. The rebuilt website carries search and generative engine optimisation infrastructure. Automated outreach powers LinkedIn messaging, email campaigns and personalised follow-ups. Advertising analysis reviews Meta, Google and TikTok campaign data to find inefficient spend, and AI-operated dashboards report bookings, leads, protocol sales, email performance and funnel analytics. The published result at nomination was more than 300% more organic search clicks, a 190% increase in impressions and more than double the Google ranking. The company now reports a later comparison, 60 days to 12 October 2026 against 9 March to 8 May 2026: clicks up more than 1,500%, more than 16 times the earlier level, impressions eight times higher, and the studio on page one rather than page four. The Singapore Business Federation checked the nomination figures at the studio in August. The stated next step is ownership, with capital going into hosting the studio's own models and infrastructure so that processing sits inside the business.

Implementation takeaway

Owning the stack is the difference between renting a capability and holding one. The gain is not the model; it is that nobody else can switch it off.

07How another SME could build something similar

Advisory, not the winner's method

This section is guidance for other companies. It is not a description of what Catalyst Performance did, and it is not endorsed by them.

Suitable for

  • · Personal training and wellness
  • · Clinics and allied health
  • · Professional services
  • · Local service businesses

Minimum requirements

  • · A website the business controls
  • · Analytics and Search Console access
  • · A written tone and claims policy, especially for health claims

Three implementation paths

Level 1

Content assistance

Draft pages and posts against real search queries, with human editing.

Use when: The site has little content.

Level 2

Assisted acquisition

Add automated follow-up and campaign analysis on top of a rebuilt site.

Use when: Enquiries arrive but conversion is weak.

Level 3

Managed digital operations

Run site, outreach, advertising and dashboards as one operating routine.

Use when: The owner wants to replace an agency retainer.

Suggested sequence

  1. 1Fix technical search basics before generating content
  2. 2Publish a small number of pages against real search demand
  3. 3Measure impressions and clicks monthly
  4. 4Add automated follow-up only after enquiry tracking works
  5. 5Review ad spend weekly with a written rule for pausing

Tools and alternatives

  • · Content management system
  • · Search Console and analytics
  • · Language model
  • · Email and messaging automation
  • · Ad platform APIs

Risks and PDPA considerations

  • · Health claims that breach advertising rules
  • · Thin content published at scale
  • · Automated outreach that breaches spam rules

KPIs to track

  • Organic clicks
  • Impressions
  • Enquiry-to-booking rate
  • Cost per booked assessment
  • Return on ad spend

08Research gaps

What remains unknown
  • The Business Insider page is a GlobeNewswire release issued by the company, and the page states that Business Insider's editorial team was not involved. It corroborates the company's account but is not independent reporting, so the grade stays at B.
  • Absolute traffic, lead and revenue figures are not published; only relative changes and the comparison period.
  • The model named Fable is not identified in any other public source. The release describes it as the latest Claude model from Anthropic.
  • The claim to be the first fitness brand to win is the company's own. The award citation lists sectors and makes no sector-first claim.
